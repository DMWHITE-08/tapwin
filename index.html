How to Play

Fill the entire 10×10 grid from 1 to 100 without getting trapped!

  • Orthogonal (↕ / ↔): Move 3 squares (skip 2 empty cells).
  • Diagonal (⤢ / ⤡): Move 2 squares (skip 1 empty cell).
  • You cannot land on an already numbered cell.
